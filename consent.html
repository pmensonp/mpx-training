FOR PARENTS & GUARDIANS

Photo, Video & Marketing Consent

MPX photographs and films lessons, camps, clinics and games so families can see progress and so we can share what we do. Because most of our players are minors, we ask a parent or guardian to choose what they're comfortable with. Every option is separate and optional, and saying no never affects your player's training.

What MPX will never do: post a player's school, home address, schedule or exact training location next to their name; share or sell your contact information; use media in a way that embarrasses or misrepresents your player; or post your player's last name without your separate OK.

Changing your mind: you can change or withdraw any permission at any time by emailing info@trainmpx.com or texting Coach Menson at (267) 678-8121. We'll take down posts featuring your player within 7 days.
